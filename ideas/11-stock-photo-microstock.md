## Premise

Use Stable Diffusion / Midjourney / FLUX to mass-generate stock photos and
upload to microstock platforms (Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, iStock, Freepik
Contributor). Earn $0.20–$2 per download.

## Why it's dropped

- Shutterstock and iStock explicitly banned "fully AI-generated" submissions
  in 2023 and have cracked down again in 2025 — most uploads get rejected.
- Payouts are tiny ($0.10–$1.50/download) and you need millions of
  impressions/month.
- Indistinguishable from the 50,000 other contributors dumping FLUX output.
  Race to the bottom on price.
- Adobe Stock's minimum payout threshold ($35) means most contributors earn
  literally $0/mo.

Even if you find a niche (medical illustrations, isometric icons), the
discoverability problem is brutal. A handful of AI contributors report
$200–$500/mo; most report $0.

## Verdict

Dropped. Mentioned only because it shows up in "make money with AI" listicles.
Real money here goes to the platforms, not to contributors.
