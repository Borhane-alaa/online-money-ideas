## Premise

Build a niche content site (language learning, indie game wiki, fan
community for a vertical, curated recipes, etc.) and monetize via
Patreon or a low-friction subscription. $3–$15/mo or $30/yr.

## How coding automates it

- Static site generator + a Patreon/Stripe integration.
- For ad revenue (B2C niche): SEO + ad network. Lower margin but bigger
  audience.
- For Patreon: curate content (LLM for drafts, human for editorial).
- For subscription: gate premium content behind auth.

## Evidence / Numbers

- **get-notes.com** (rubymamis, HN 35567822): OSS note-taking app w/
  $2k/mo **from ads on landing page** (organic SEO). 1.2M downloads.
- **jpdb.io** (kouteiheika, HN 35567822): Japanese-learning site,
  **$1996/mo from Patreon**, 2+ years to that level. "Not aggressively
  monetized."
- **Kpopping.com** (p3rls, HN 35567822): K-pop fan platform, 6 years to
  $3k–$5k/mo at $30/yr.
- **DamnInteresting.com** (DamnInteresting, HN 35567822): 17 years to
  consistent $2k/mo (gross).

## Cold-start plan

1. Pick a niche you have authentic interest in.
2. Ship 30+ pages of content manually in month 1.
3. Add a Patreon or $3/mo subscription gate.
4. SEO compounds; 18-month horizon is realistic.
5. Optional: ads for non-paying audience, subscription for power users.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Slow burn**: most niche sites need 12–24 months before any
  meaningful revenue. Don't quit the day job.
- **Burnout**: solo content creator with a family is brutal.
- **Platform lock-in**: Patreon takes 5–12% plus payment fees. Stripe +
  a tiny auth flow is cheaper at scale.
- **Ad RPM volatility**: get-notes.com's $25.80 CPM was an outlier —
  most ad-supported sites earn cents per visitor.

## Verdict

Validate next as a side project. Slow but real revenue, very low
maintenance once content base exists. Best paired with a more active
micro-SaaS as a hedge.
