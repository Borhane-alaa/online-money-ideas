## Premise

Curate and sell bundles of high-quality prompts for a specific profession
("prompts for solo lawyers", "prompts for indie game writers", "prompts for
grant-writing for non-profits"). Price $19–$79 per pack on Gumroad / Lemon
Squeezy.

Examples: `godofprompt.ai`, `promptbase.com`, hundreds of Gumroad listings.

## How coding automates it

- Compile the prompts once, sell forever. Maintenance near zero.
- Gumroad handles fulfillment. Optional: build a small web app that wraps
  the prompts behind a nicer UX.

## Evidence / Numbers

- PromptBase: $1M+ GMV over the lifetime of the marketplace, but median
  seller earns <$500 total.
- Top Gumroad listings ("100 ChatGPT prompts for X") net $1k–$10k in first
  60 days then decay.

## Cold-start plan

1. Pick a niche where you actually use prompts.
2. Build a pack of 50–100 prompts, organized by use case.
3. Make 1 demo of each generating a useful artifact (Notion DB, doc,
   email).
4. List on Gumroad. Promote on Twitter/Reddit.
5. If traction, ship 2 more packs; build a bundle.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Shelf life**: as foundation models improve, prompt packs lose value
  quickly. Plans for the format to die within 12–18 months.
- **Piracy** — once a PDF is in the wild, it's in the wild. Mostly solved
  by bundling into an app or a Notion template, not raw text.
- **Buyer sophistication**: serious buyers buy the *system*, not prompts.
  Move to templates + Notion DBs as you iterate.

## Verdict

Validate next as a low-risk side project. Don't build the business on it —
treat revenue as a signal of where your actual niche interest is.
