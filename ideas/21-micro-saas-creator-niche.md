## Premise

Build a tiny paid SaaS for one specific creator / professional workflow:
Lightroom preset reverse-engineering, custom jewelry preview, niche asset
management, color picker, font browser, etc. $1.99–$9.99/mo.

## How coding automates it

- Single-purpose app + Stripe + minimal dashboard.
- Often goes viral once on a relevant subreddit or Product Hunt.
- Maintenance burden is the lowest of any micro-SaaS (narrow feature set).

## Evidence / Numbers

- **PixelPeeper** (pchm, HN 35567822): "took a month or two to reach
  $2k/mo" via Instagram virality, has run for 6+ years solo, still
  profitable but capped because niche is small.
- **PlaylistAI** (bbauman, HN 35567822): iOS prompt → playlist app,
  $2.5k MRR, 2 weeks to $2k after launch.
- **Epidemic Sound / Artlist competitors** all started as creator tools.

## Cold-start plan

1. Pick a creator/professional workflow you already use.
2. Find the #1 repetitive pain point. Build a one-purpose tool for it.
3. Charge $1.99–$9.99/mo. Or $19 one-time.
4. Launch on the relevant subreddit + Product Hunt + your network.

## Risks & gotchas

- **Niche ceiling**: PixelPeeper's founder explicitly says the niche was
  too small to grow past $2k/mo for years. Plan for this.
- **Platform dependency**: if Instagram dies, PixelPeeper dies.
- **Differentiation**: the niche you pick may have a free tool already,
  or get one in 12 months.

## Verdict

Validate next. Best entry point for "I have an idea but no time" —
these can be weekend projects that hit $500–$2k/mo and stay there. The
ceiling is the ceiling, but the floor is friendly. Pair this with one of
the bigger bets.
