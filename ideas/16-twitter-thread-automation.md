## Premise

Operate a niche Twitter/X account that posts daily threads on one topic
(LLM debugging tactics, Postgres performance, macOS shell tricks, indie
Saas lessons, retrocomputing, etc.). Build audience. Monetize via
sponsorships, affiliate links, or as a funnel to your SaaS.

## How coding automates it

- LLM drafts the threads from curated sources (HN, RSS, arXiv).
- Editorial pass: trim, fact-check, adjust voice.
- Schedule via Typefully / Buffer / custom.
- Track engagement, double down on the formats that work.

## Evidence / Numbers

- Top niche accounts ("@levelsio", "@marc_louvion", "@dvassallo", dozens of
  "indie founder" accounts): sponsorships $500–$10k/post once they cross
  ~20k followers.
- Mid-tier accounts (5k–20k): $50–$500/post.
- Most niche accounts: $0/mo — they don't reach the sponsorship bar.

## Cold-start plan

1. Pick a niche where you can post 7 days a week for a year without
   burning out.
2. Write 30 threads manually to lock the voice.
3. Automate the draft + schedule.
4. Engage manually with the niche community 30 min/day.
5. Sponsorships at 10k followers (6–18 months).

## Risks & gotchas

- **Twitter TOS**: aggressive automation gets rate-limited and accounts
  suspended. Keep human in the loop.
- **Engagement bait decay**: the algorithm punishes predictable formats.
- **One-platform risk**: if your audience lives on X and X dies, your revenue
  dies. Diversify to a newsletter (see idea #06).

## Verdict

Validate next only if you have one of these already — niche expertise,
time, or existing audience. Otherwise treat as a side experiment to build
credibility for idea #01 or #06.
